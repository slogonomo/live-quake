The Ironwail patch turns Ironwail into a shared engine that the same frontend loads:
- it renders OpenGL into GPU buffers (GBM/EGL) and hands them to the viewer as dmabufs, with no per-frame CPU copy;
- it hooks the engine into the same control socket, exact suspension, input ownership, bot and native checkpoints;
- it sends sound to the frontend instead of opening a device itself;
- it never loads `ironwail.pak` (see below).

Distributing a built engine means also offering its corresponding source:
- the pinned Ironwail revision below;
- the matching patch;
- `src/ironwail/*` (except the development-only `lq_egl_offscreen.c`) and the shared exit parser `src/level_bypass.h`
  (GPL-2.0-or-later);
- the matching 0BSD frontend, bridge and GPU transport sources and headers.

Nothing here relicenses Quake or LibreQuake data.

## Settings menu art

The settings menu ships **no** font, picture or sound. `menu/pakart.py` reads the console font, the OPTIONS plaque,
the message-box frame and the three menu sounds from **your own** game data at runtime. It caches them privately
(`0700`) for display on your screen only, and never redistributes them.
